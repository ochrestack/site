Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy covers the Ochrestack website at ochrestack.com and the software tools we publish. It is written to be read.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics scripts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and operation.

Our tools

Our tools are built to work without sending your data to us. Unless a tool's own documentation says otherwise, it does not collect, transmit, or store personal data, and it contains no analytics or tracking. Where a tool must contact a service to do its job, for example to check a license key or to call the platform it runs inside, its documentation says exactly what is sent and why.

Purchases

Purchases made directly from us are processed by our merchant of record, Polar Software Inc., which handles payment, invoicing, and applicable taxes under its own privacy policy. Purchases made through a platform marketplace, such as Atlassian, JetBrains, Odoo, Wix, or Superhive, are processed by that platform under its policies. We receive what is necessary to deliver and support what you bought, typically your email address and your order details.

Email

If you subscribe to updates, we store your email address and send you only what you signed up for. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and requests are honored promptly. We never sell or rent email addresses.

Support

When you write to us, we keep the correspondence so we can help you and improve the tools. We do not share it.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@ochrestack.com and we will respond within 30 days. Residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California have additional rights under their local laws, and we honor them.

Changes
