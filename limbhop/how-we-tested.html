How Limbhop was tested

Back to the Limbhop product page. All numbers are Limbhop 1.0.1, on a 4-core Intel Xeon at 2.1 GHz, Linux. Movement is the largest move of any deforming bone during a switch. Check Limb's tolerance is 1.0 mm per metre of limb length and 0.5 degrees.

WhatResult
Headless tests, Blender 4.5.16 LTS, 5.2.4 LTS, 5.3.096 tests on each: 90, 95 and 95 passed, none failed; 6, 1 and 1 skipped (five CloudRig tests need Blender 5.0; the undo test needs a window).
Real window, real key presses and clicks126 steps on 5.2.4, 119 on 4.5.16, 118 on 5.3.0; none failed.
Rigify human (MPFB base mesh, CC0; Rigify 0.6.10), every arm and leg both ways, Blender 5.2.4Limbhop 0.009 mm. Rigify's own IK->FK button plus slider up to 1.03 mm and 0.17 degrees.
CloudRig human (CloudRig 2.2.32)Limbhop 0.0018 mm. CloudRig's own Snap & Bake up to 0.034 mm.
Ellie's arms, FK to IK, five frames of her animationLimbhop 0.30 mm. Her own Snap & Bake, which runs on Blender 4.5, up to 5.76 mm and 12.82 degrees.
Ellie, IK to FKHer corrective bones move up to 18.1 mm with Limbhop and with her own Snap & Bake alike: over the tolerance, and Check Limb reports it.
Actions per switch, counted by doing them in a real windowRigify's own route 6, CloudRig's Snap & Bake 3, Limbhop 1.
