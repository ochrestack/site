Ochrestack

Small, well-made tools for the platforms you already use.

Ochrestack is an independent software studio founded in 2026. We build focused tools that fix one real problem well, for Atlassian, JetBrains, Odoo, WordPress, Wix, Windows, Blender, and the major browsers. Every tool ships with documentation, tests, and a support address that answers.

Products

Rigbake for Blender: one clean, correctly named animation clip per action, with presets for Godot (checked in Godot 4.7), Unity and Unreal (not yet opened in Unity or Unreal). Fixes empty or duplicated exports from control rigs and NLA clips. Source ships with purchase.

Actionyard for Blender: lists every action in the file with every place that uses it (object, NLA track, Action Stash, Action constraint, with the slot), and clears the Action Stash tracks you pick that Purge won't remove, in one previewed, undoable step. Blender 4.4 and later; $12, every future version included. Source ships with purchase.

Exportmint for Blender: re-exports only the Collection Exporters that changed, says why each stale one is out of date, and writes a manifest of every run (written, unchanged, failed, with sizes and SHA-256), also from the command line across several .blend files. Blender 4.2 LTS and later; $12, every future version included. Source ships with purchase.

Skintap for Blender: click your posed character, body or clothing, and it selects the bone that moves that spot, with no setup; copy and mirror bone chains with names that count up instead of .001. Blender 4.2 LTS and later; $14, 12 months of updates included. Source ships with purchase.

Proptidy for Blender: keeps custom properties in the order you set, in the Properties editor, the Sidebar and every shot file that links the rig, with no renaming, and edits Min, Max, default and tooltip in fields below the list instead of a pop-up. Blender 4.2 LTS to 5.3; $6 once, every future update included. Source ships with purchase.

Sunreckon for Blender: Blender's own physical sky and a matching Sun light from a real place, date and clock time in one click, with the exposure set, four cloud presets with their measured render cost, and your HDRIs in the same tab. Blender 4.2 LTS to 5.2 LTS, tested on each; $19 once, updates for the life of the product. Source ships with purchase.

Spallwork for Blender: chipped, worn and ruined edges on closed meshes as real geometry, on many objects at once. Separate bricks stay separate, chip size is in metres on scaled objects, and Revert brings the original back even after Apply. Blender 4.5 LTS to 5.2 LTS, tested on each; $15 once, updates at no charge. Source ships with purchase.

Wholegrip for Blender: makes a collection you already have work like one object. One click selects all of it, child collections included; one modifier stack stays in step on every part, Geometry Nodes inputs too; one Outliner entry includes or excludes it in every view layer. Blender 4.2 LTS to 5.2 LTS, tested on 4.2, 4.5, 5.0, 5.1 and 5.2 (Linux); $12 once, every future version included. Source ships with purchase.

Attachkeep for Confluence: finds the Confluence Cloud attachments no page, blog post, comment or diagram on your site refers to, including uploads from people who have left, and moves the ones you choose to the space trash after a preview and a dry run. Free for up to 10 users; coming to the Atlassian Marketplace.

Labelkeep for Jira: lists every label on your Jira Cloud site with its issue count and groups duplicate spellings, then deletes, renames or merges labels on every issue the app can access, with a preview and a dry run first, in the background, naming Jira's reason and the fix for any issue that fails. Free; coming to the Atlassian Marketplace.

Sortlatch for Confluence: keeps a Confluence Cloud page's child pages in A to Z order, permanently. New, renamed and moved pages go to their place within minutes, with numbers in natural order, Z to A, newest or oldest first, pins, a preview and undo. Free for up to 10 users; coming to the Atlassian Marketplace.

Xsdtrail for XSD and WSDL: a Design tab for XML Schema and WSDL files in IntelliJ IDEA and other JetBrains IDEs (2025.1 and later). A schema split across many files opens as one diagram, each box labelled with its file, and a double-click opens the declaration. Free for one file; $12 once for multi-file schemas and the full WSDL operation view. Coming to JetBrains Marketplace; not submitted yet.

Hexgrain: Debugger Hex View: a Java or Kotlin byte array in the IntelliJ IDEA debugger as a hex dump, with offsets, 8, 16 or 32 bytes per row and the characters beside them, and the bytes that changed since the last pause marked. IntelliJ IDEA 2025.3 and later. Free up to 64 KB; $9 once for arrays of any size, more types, a data inspector, Find, Copy As and Save. Source code included. Coming to JetBrains Marketplace; listing in review.

Histsight: Merge & Amend: fast-forward-only and always-a-merge-commit merges in the Branches popup of JetBrains IDEs (2025.1 and later), with a hint before you click, and a warning before you amend a commit a remote branch already contains: both free. $9 once adds a preview of which commits a rebase will stop on, and in which files, and message-only or author amends, also with several repositories. Source code included. Coming to JetBrains Marketplace; listing in review.

Scrollgrep: Terminal Filter: a live filter for the Terminal tool window of IntelliJ IDEA and other JetBrains IDEs (2025.3 and later). Type text or a grep -E pattern and a panel beside the Terminal lists only the matching lines, adding new ones as the output scrolls; the Terminal itself is left as it was. Free to filter; $12 once for clickable paths and stack frames, one-command scope with context, colour-coded patterns saved per project and watch alerts. Coming to JetBrains Marketplace; listing in review.

Linkrounds for WordPress: a broken link checker that runs on your own server, with no account and no link limit, and calls a link Broken only after it fails twice, an hour apart. Every fix is a post revision you can restore. Free; Linkrounds Pro adds scheduled scans, an email report and CSV export for $19 one-time (up to 3 sites), no renewal.

Linkhither for Confluence: shows which pages and blog posts link to a Confluence Cloud page, on the page itself: an Incoming links macro, a count under every title, and a report in every space with CSV, each list checked against what the reader may open. Free for up to 10 users; coming to the Atlassian Marketplace.

Restorwell for WordPress: restores a .wpress backup made with All-in-One WP Migration, free, past your host's upload limit, with or without a password, after checking the whole file and your server's limits. Free; Restorwell Pro adds Restore from a link for $19 once, unlimited sites, no renewal. Not affiliated with All-in-One WP Migration or ServMask.
