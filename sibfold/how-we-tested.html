How we tested Sibfold

Back to the product page. Everything here is version 1.0.2 and ran on Linux.

  • 157 automated tests, none failing, run without a display.
  • 340 steps in 32 scripted runs in full IDE windows (IntelliJ IDEA and PyCharm, 2025.2 and 2026.3, under a virtual display), none failing. Scripted clicks and keys; no person drove them.
  • Plugin Verifier on 15 builds (IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, WebStorm, GoLand, PhpStorm, RubyMine and RustRover 2025.2 and 2026.3, Android Studio 2025.2): all Compatible, no internal API used.
  • gRPC Go at commit 50ea134, counted from the Project view's tree in IntelliJ IDEA 2025.2: the IDE's one rule .go with _test.go nests 225 files; the free rule on the product page nests 266, the same 225 and 41 more such as server_ext_test.go; three suffix rules typed into the IDE's dialog nest 241; the Go ready set nests 282. That is 41 of the 1,221 files at the top level of its folders.
  • On nine public projects the ready sets added files on five (41, 6, 40, 9 and 35) and none on four. What you gain depends on how many of your names have a variable middle.
  • Speed, median of 10 runs in a window in IntelliJ IDEA 2025.2 (Linux, 4 CPUs): gRPC Go's largest folder, 53 files with three Go rules, 1.4 ms with Sibfold on and 1.0 ms off; a generated folder of 5,001 files with 20 rules, 0.80 s to work out after a change. One folder of 10,000 Go files takes about 4 s and one of 20,000 about 18 s (names only, best of 3).
  • The pictures on the product page are captures from IntelliJ IDEA 2025.2 started with the plugin built from source, on clones of gRPC Go and Bootstrap 4.6.2. The pictures of the paid features were taken with the paid features switched on by a test tool; the Marketplace trial and purchase have not been run.
