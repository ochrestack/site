Heedpath – Notification Checker for Jira

When someone says “I never got the email”, Heedpath shows a Jira Cloud admin whether the notification scheme and permissions send that event to them, and the line that decides it.

  • “Why didn't they get the email?” Notified, Not notified or Cannot tell, and why.
  • “Some team members are notified and some not.” Who gets this lists everyone one event reaches, and who drops out, and why.

How it works

  1. Pick an issue, a person and an event.
  2. Heedpath reads the scheme, roles, groups, watchers, issue security and the account. It changes nothing.
  3. One answer, with the line that decides it, ready to copy.

What Jira already does, since Atlassian removed the Notification Helper. Jira already lets an admin find the answer by hand: open the project's notification scheme, read the event's row, then check the person against each entry (role members on the People page, group members in user management, the issue's watchers, custom field values), then check their access with the Permission helper. Atlassian's own recommendation since the 2024 removal is to log in as the person and look at their notification settings. For one simple case on a small site that is enough. Heedpath does the whole chain in one answer.

Known limits

Reads configuration, not deliveries; every answer lists what it cannot see. Company-managed projects, Jira admins, English. Quick start.
