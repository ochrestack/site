How it works

  1. Pick an issue, a person and an event.
  2. Heedpath reads the scheme, roles, groups, watchers, issue security and the account. It changes nothing.
  3. One answer, with the line that decides it, ready to copy.

What Jira already does

Jira already lets an admin find the answer by hand: open the project's notification scheme, read the event's row, then check the person against each entry (role members on the People page, group members in user management, the issue's watchers, custom field values), then check their access with the Permission helper. Atlassian's own recommendation since the 2024 removal is to log in as the person and look at their notification settings. For one simple case on a small site that is enough. Heedpath does the whole chain in one answer, and names customer and deactivated accounts and those without Jira access.

Known limits

Reads configuration, not deliveries; every answer lists what it cannot see. Not for emails to Jira Service Management portal customers: those follow the service project's Customer notifications, which Heedpath does not read. Company-managed projects only: team-managed projects get Cannot tell, with the reason. Jira admins, English. Quick start.
