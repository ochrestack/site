Rigbake Check: support

Rigbake Check is a free Blender extension that checks a rig before game export and lists each problem with a one-line fix. If it reports something wrong, misses a problem, or one of its fixes (Apply Scale, Limit Weights) does something you did not expect, tell us.

How to report a problem

Email support@ochrestack.com with "Rigbake Check" in the subject, and include:

  1. Your Blender version (Help > About Blender) and your operating system.
  2. The Rigbake Check version (Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions, under Installed).
  3. What the dialog says: the lines of the check dialog, or the full report it opens in the Text Editor (copy the text, or attach a screenshot).
  4. What you expected instead, and what you did just before (for example, which fix button you clicked).
  5. The .blend file, if you can share it. A cut-down copy that still shows the problem is enough. We use it only to reproduce the problem and delete it when the report is closed.

What happens next

We answer every message, usually within one business day. If we can reproduce the problem, the fix goes into the next release on extensions.blender.org, and we tell you when it is out.

Privacy

Rigbake Check makes no network calls and sends nothing anywhere; the only data we receive is what you email us. See the privacy notice.
