How Texapart was tested

What was run, on what, and what came out. Back to the Texapart product page.

SceneResult
Rain, packed as the product page describes (Texapart 1.0.6, Blender 4.2.23)1 material, 6 images, 20,851 faces: all placed in 6 one-image materials, 0 listed, 0.33 s. A Cycles render (32 samples, 640 x 800) before and after: mean difference 0.0%, no pixel differs. A control that shows her hair image on her jeans measures 0.80%, so the comparison does see a wrong image. Exported with Blender's glTF and FBX exporters and re-imported: 6 materials, each with one image.
Barcelona Pavilion, rebuilt as an importer delivers it (Texapart 1.0.6, Blender 5.2.4)3 multi-image materials on 7 objects became 8 one-image materials; 7,480 faces placed in 0.2 s; 1 face listed, not guessed (its UVs sit 0.0024 outside its UDIM tile), placed with Move Selected Faces. Render difference 0.0%. In Godot 4.7: one albedo texture per surface.
Scenes built to break itThe same image at two scales, two UV maps, colour adjustments, unlit shaders, unclamped mixes, a fixed 50% mix, material indices past the last slot, linked duplicates outside the selection, Array modifiers with UV offsets: 25 scenarios; in each, every face either keeps its look or is listed.
Automated tests, real Blender, headless194 passed and 1 skipped in each of Blender 4.2.23, 4.5.16, 5.2.4 and 5.3.0 (Linux). The skipped one is undo; Ctrl+Z after the split was checked with real key presses on 4.2.23 and 5.2.4.
Size204,800 faces on 40 objects: Report and Split each under 0.9 s. 2,000,000 faces on 200 objects: Report 4.6 to 5.4 s, Split 8.5 to 10.7 s (Texapart 1.0.6, 4-core Linux VM).

The demo film

The demo film and the 14-second loop were recorded with Texapart 1.0.5 on Rain; the pictures of the panel, the Texapart panel loop at the top of the page and the Pavilion Report were captured with 1.0.6. Texapart 1.0.6 words the panel differently in two places the film shows: above the Report list the film has two lines of counts ("1 material with more than one image", "0 faces listed") where 1.0.6 has one line, "Report list (selected objects)", because the Result box already says what Report found; and after a split the film's list holds one "Rain_Game: done" row and a hint to click a row where 1.0.6 says "Rain_Game: split into one-image materials. Nothing left to split." What the film shows Texapart doing to Rain is the same in 1.0.6: the same Rain run places the same 20,851 faces in the same six materials (table above). The film's Godot shot shows the glTF of the split Rain, and Godot 4.7 reads the export of 1.0.6 the same way: six surfaces, one albedo texture each. In the Godot windows the interface words are small on a phone (about 6 to 7 px on a 360 px player); the captions carry what they show. Not run: Windows, macOS, Unity and Unreal.
