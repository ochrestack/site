How Texapart was tested

What was run, on what, and what came out. Back to the Texapart product page.

SceneResult
Rain, packed as the product page describes (Blender 4.2.23)1 material, 6 images, 20,851 faces: all placed in 6 one-image materials, 0 listed, 0.25 s. A Cycles render (32 samples, 640 x 800) before and after: mean difference 0.0%, no pixel differs. A control that shows her hair image on her jeans measures 0.80%, so the comparison does see a wrong image. Exported with Blender's glTF and FBX exporters and re-imported: 6 materials, each with one image.
Barcelona Pavilion, rebuilt as an importer delivers it (Blender 5.2.4)3 multi-image materials on 7 objects became 8 one-image materials; 7,480 faces placed in 0.2 s; 1 face listed, not guessed (its UVs sit 0.0024 outside its UDIM tile), placed with Move Selected Faces. Render difference 0.0%. In Godot 4.7: one albedo texture per surface.
Scenes built to break itThe same image at two scales, two UV maps, colour adjustments, unlit shaders, unclamped mixes, a fixed 50% mix, material indices past the last slot, linked duplicates outside the selection, Array modifiers with UV offsets: 25 scenarios; in each, every face either keeps its look or is listed.
Automated tests, real Blender, headless132 passed and 1 skipped in each of Blender 4.2.23, 4.5.16, 5.2.4 and 5.3.0 (Linux). The skipped one is undo; Ctrl+Z after the split was checked with real key presses on 4.2.23 and 5.2.4.
Size204,800 faces on 40 objects: Report and Split each under 0.8 s. 2,000,000 faces on 200 objects: Report 3.9 to 4.4 s, Split 7.1 to 8.7 s (4-core Linux VM).
