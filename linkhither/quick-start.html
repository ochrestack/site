Linkhither quick start

Installing Linkhither for Confluence and using its four screens: the Incoming links macro, the count under each title, the space report and the admin page. Back to the Linkhither product page.

Install (Confluence Cloud)

  1. In Confluence, open Apps > Explore more apps (or Settings > Find new apps), search for Linkhither for Confluence and choose Get app. You need to be a site or Confluence admin. The app can be found there once its Marketplace listing is live.
  2. Review the permissions and confirm. The product page lists each permission and what it is for.
  3. Indexing starts by itself. There is nothing to set up.

The first index

Open Settings > Linkhither in Confluence (Confluence admins only). It shows:

  • pages and blog posts read out of those found, with a progress bar that refreshes while indexing runs;
  • when the index last changed, and when the last page event was handled;
  • how many pages and blog posts the app was not permitted to read, and why: spaces closed to apps (for example by an app access rule), pages you can open but the app cannot because page restrictions leave it out, and pages that were listed but could not be read. The restricted-page check goes space by space, a few seconds at a time, and says how many spaces it has checked.

Until the first index finishes, lists may be incomplete. On our test site it read 108 to 517 ms per page or blog post, so 10,000 pages take very roughly 20 to 90 minutes. Rebuild index reads everything again; only Confluence admins can use it.

Put the list on a page: the Incoming links macro

  1. Edit a page, type /Incoming links, and press Enter.
  2. Optionally, open the macro's settings (the pencil icon) to:
    • show links from all spaces, this space only, or these spaces only (space keys, separated by commas);
    • show only pages and blog posts with a label;
    • choose what readers see when nothing links here: a message you can edit, or nothing at all.
  3. Publish. Readers see a table of the linking pages and blog posts: title (linked), space, and last updated, with a Blog post marker on blog posts. Click Page or blog post or Last updated to sort; click again to reverse.

The count under every title

Under the title of every page and blog post, Linkhither adds "N incoming links" ("1 incoming link" when there is one). Click it to open the same list in a popup.

The space report and CSV

  1. In any space, open Incoming links report under Apps in the space sidebar. It loads in steps and shows how far it has got.
  2. Each page in the space that you can view is listed with its number of incoming links, the pages and blog posts that link to it (with their space key), and its last update. Pages that pull it in with Include Page or Excerpt Include are marked includes this page; they are not counted as links.
  3. Tick Only pages with no incoming links to see the pages that no other page links to or includes. Click a column header to sort.
  4. Click Download CSV. The file has exactly the rows and columns shown, with your filter and sort applied, as UTF-8 that Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets open directly. Pages that include a page appear in the "Linked from" column, marked "[includes this page]".

Before you archive a page, also check its Page information in Confluence: Linkhither does not see links held in other macros' settings, such as Children Display.

Which links count

  • Counted: page links made with the link picker, with or without a space key; pasted page URLs, full or short (/wiki/x/…), and old viewpage.action links; smart links in inline, card or embed view; and any of these inside panels, expands and other macro bodies. Links copied from the Share dialog (/l/cp/…) are counted when Confluence redirects them to the page for the app; this is not yet checked on a real site.
  • Not counted: links held in other macros' settings (Include Page, Excerpt Include, Children Display); links in comments, Jira or outside websites; links from live docs, whiteboards, databases and folders; a page's links to itself.

Limits

  • New and changed links appear within a few minutes, not instantly: Atlassian delivers page events to apps up to 3 minutes after an edit. An hourly check picks up any change whose event never arrived.
  • Pages the app is not permitted to read are not indexed, even for readers who may open them. The admin page shows how many and why.
  • Each list is checked as the reader, so two people can see different counts on the same page.
  • Guests and anonymous visitors see neither the macro nor the byline.
  • The space report lists pages, not blog posts; blog posts still appear as linking content. Archived pages and drafts are not counted as linking content.
  • In Word and PDF exports, the macro prints a one-line note instead of the list.
  • Confluence Cloud only; no Data Center or Server. English only.

Privacy

Linkhither runs on Atlassian Forge and talks only to your own Confluence site. It sends nothing anywhere else and has no analytics. It stores ids and which page links to which, never page content, titles or account ids. Details on the product page and in the privacy policy.
